# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors, maintainers, and users of the GovUX Audit Platform — pledge to
make participation a harassment-free, respectful, and professional experience for
everyone, regardless of role, seniority, background, or ability. This is a
government platform built to make public services more accessible and inclusive;
we hold our own conduct to that same standard.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming, inclusive, and plain language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the platform's users — India's citizens.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing sensitive government data, credentials, or audit findings outside
  authorised channels.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional government setting.

## Responsibilities & enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take fair corrective
action in response to unacceptable behaviour, including removing contributions or
revoking access.

Report concerns confidentially to the project maintainers or your programme's
designated ethics/HR contact. All reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly.
