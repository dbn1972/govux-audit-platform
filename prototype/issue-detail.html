Payment step is not operable by keyboard

● CriticalAccessibilityMedium effortAffects 3 pages

What's wrong

On the certificate application, the “Proceed to pay” control is a <div> with a click handler. It cannot receive keyboard focus, so citizens who navigate with a keyboard or assistive technology — including many persons with disabilities — reach the payment step and cannot proceed. This blocks task completion entirely.

Why it matters to citizens

Keyboard operability is a Level A requirement — the most basic tier of accessibility and a legal obligation under GIGW 3.0 and the RPwD Act, 2016. A blocked payment step means affected citizens must visit a physical office, defeating the purpose of the digital service.

🛡This is a guard-rail issue: it caps the whole site at Band C until fixed.

Evidence

Captured during audit
Rendered element · /apply?step=pay
<div class="btn-pay" onclick="pay()">Proceed to pay</div>

How to fix ✓

Use a native <button> (focusable and keyboard-operable by default), or add role, tabindex and key handlers.

<!-- Recommended --> <button type="submit" class="btn-pay">Proceed to pay</button>
